## 4. Logo Direction & Geometry

The NEXORA symbol combines:
1. **Stylized Letter N**: Constructed with razor-sharp geometric precision.
2. **Two Connected Device Nodes**: A top-left sender node (Electric Blue `#3978F6`) and bottom-right receiver node (Signal Teal `#39C6B0`).
3. **Dynamic Data-Transfer Path**: An active diagonal stream bridging the two nodes with transfer data packets.
4. **Sense of Movement**: Forward-leaning connection and direct encrypted communication.

## 5. Strict Branding Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - Never use names like "Universal Nearby" or generic "P2P Share". NEXORA is the sole official name.
> - Do not use emojis as the primary product logo.
> - Maintain contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for standard text and 3:1 for large graphical elements (WCAG AA).
> - Never advertise unreleased features or fake transfer metrics.
